MakineNabız Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026
App: MakineNabız (com.minifun.mqtt_control_system)
Contact: tuncaytrkylmz1@gmail.com

1. Overview

MakineNabız is an Android app that measures machine/motor vibration, sound and frequency using phone sensors. This policy explains how data is handled.

2. Data we process

Data is stored locally on your device:

  • Equipment names and settings
  • Measurement results (vibration, frequency, sound level, etc.)
  • Optional equipment photos
  • PDF header details you enter (logo, company name)
  • Language preference

No account is required. No advertising or analytics SDKs are included.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone: ambient sound level (dB) measurement.
  • Camera: equipment photos.
  • Notifications: system notifications (Android 13+).

4. Sharing

Measurement and equipment data are not sold or shared with third parties by default. If you share a PDF or photo yourself, that sharing is under your control.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays on device storage. You can delete equipment/measurements in the app or uninstall the app.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
